A tired website can need a new coat of paint or a new foundation. The two cost very different amounts, so it’s worth deciding carefully.

## A redesign is probably enough if…

- The content is mostly right; it just looks dated.
- Your team can update it without help.
- It’s reasonably fast and works on phones.

## A rebuild is worth it if…

- Every small change needs a developer.
- It’s slow, breaks on mobile, or fails basic accessibility checks.
- The structure no longer matches what you do today.
- It’s built on something nobody wants to maintain.

## A quick test

Open your site on your phone, on mobile data, and try to do the one thing a new visitor most needs to do. Time it. If it takes longer than a minute or makes you wince, that tells you more than any audit.

---

Whichever it is, we start with content and structure, not colors. A beautiful redesign of the wrong thing is still the wrong thing.
